Phizzle Edge Puck

  • Purpose-Built for Cleanrooms: Compact, cleanable, and malware-hardened gateway built for regulated environments
  • Extend the Lifespan of Legacy Instruments: Converts existing lab devices into connected data nodes while supporting audit trail and data integrity
  • Protects Against Data-Loss: Maintain continuous data capture even during network failures and power outages
Learn More

Connected Plant Platform

  • Synchronize Data Directly with Backend Systems: Automatically push instrument data to your existing LIMS, MES, or QMS
  • Manage Instruments Remotely: Configure and monitor multi-vendor analytical instruments from a single administrator interface
  • Query Audit Logs and Results Instantly: Access a fully searchable, time-stamped record of all instrument data and transactions for compliance and reporting
Learn More

Instrument Connectors

  • Standardize Multi-Vendor Outputs: Our connectors standardize the communication idiosyncrasies between vendors, so your IT team isn't burdened maintaining one-off integrations
  • Different Connectors for Different Digitization Visions: From simple validated data extraction to fully automated sample initiation and format standardization
  • Support Compliance Automatically: Every result includes a detailed audit log, chain of custody tracking, and built-in 21 CFR Part 11 e-sign workflows
